Cover Screen OS bundles two offline word-frequency corpora for keyboard
autocorrect and predictive text:

  - assets/english_words.bin  — unigram corpus (82,765 words), "word frequency" per line, gzip-compressed.
  - assets/english_bigrams.bin — bigram corpus (top 60,000 by frequency), "word1 word2 frequency" per line, gzip-compressed.

Both are trimmed from the demo data bundled with the SymSpellKt library
(https://github.com/Darkrock-Studios/SymSpellKt), which traces its lineage
to Wolf Garbe's original SymSpell project. All are MIT licensed:
